# Scene Image Prompting Guide\n\nCraft detailed prompts for generating consistent, high-quality scene reference images.\n\n## Prompt Structure\n\n```\n[Art Style] [Subject], [Environment], [Composition], [Lighting].\n[Action/Emotion]. [Duration]. Aspect ratio: [9:16 or 16:9].\nResolution: 720p. [Additional style notes].\n```\n\n## Core Components\n\n### 1. Art Style (Critical for Consistency)\n\nBe specific and consistent across all scenes:\n\n**Chibi/Cute**\n- \"Cute chibi cartoon style\"\n- \"Round heads, tiny bodies, huge sparkling eyes, rosy cheeks\"\n- \"2D anime chibi art style, Studio Ghibli inspired\"\n\n**Realistic**\n- \"Photorealistic style\"\n- \"Professional wildlife photography\"\n- \"Cinematic realism with warm lighting\"\n\n**Watercolor/Painted**\n- \"Watercolor illustration style\"\n- \"Soft pastel colors, delicate brushstrokes\"\n- \"Hand-painted aesthetic\"\n\n**3D/CGI**\n- \"3D rendered style\"\n- \"Pixar-inspired 3D animation\"\n- \"High-poly 3D modeling\"\n\n### 2. Subject Description\n\nInclude:\n- **Character**: Species, size, pose, expression, clothing/accessories\n- **Quantity**: Single, multiple, group\n- **Details**: Fur texture, eye color, unique features\n\n**Example**: \"One adorable chubby chibi giant panda sitting cross-legged, holding a thick bamboo stalk with both tiny paws, taking a big happy bite with rosy cheeks and sparkling eyes\"\n\n### 3. Environment\n\nDescribe:\n- **Location**: Bamboo forest, mountain, city, fantasy world\n- **Scale**: Vast landscape, intimate scene, overhead view\n- **Weather/Time**: Sunny, rainy, night, golden hour, misty\n- **Vegetation/Objects**: Trees, flowers, water, structures\n\n**Example**: \"Lush, dense bamboo forest with vibrant green, thick tall bamboo stalks everywhere, sunlight filtering through canopy creating dappled light\"\n\n### 4. Composition\n\nSpecify:\n- **Camera Angle**: Close-up, medium shot, wide shot, overhead, low angle\n- **Depth**: Foreground/background layers, focal point\n- **Framing**: Centered, rule of thirds, asymmetric\n- **Perspective**: First-person, third-person, isometric\n\n**Example**: \"Close-up of panda in center frame, bamboo forest blurred in background, shallow depth of field focusing on face\"\n\n### 5. Lighting\n\nDescribe:\n- **Light Source**: Sun, moon, artificial, multiple sources\n- **Quality**: Soft, harsh, diffused, dramatic\n- **Color Temperature**: Warm (golden), cool (blue), neutral\n- **Shadows**: Pronounced, subtle, none\n\n**Example**: \"Warm natural sunlight, soft shadows, golden hour lighting with warm color tones\"\n\n### 6. Action/Emotion\n\nConvey:\n- **Mood**: Happy, playful, peaceful, excited, curious\n- **Movement**: Static, dynamic, mid-action\n- **Expression**: Smiling, surprised, content, laughing\n\n**Example**: \"Looking extremely satisfied and joyful, surrounded by piles of bamboo everywhere\"\n\n## Complete Example Prompts\n\n### Scene 1: Close-up Eating\n```\nCute chibi cartoon style, one adorable chubby giant panda sitting, \nholding a thick bamboo stalk with both tiny paws, taking a big happy bite \nwith rosy cheeks and sparkling eyes. One rolling on a pile of bamboo leaves laughing, \none climbing a bamboo stalk with a goofy grin, one sitting on a \nbamboo platform eating bamboo happily. Colorful flowers on the ground. \nBamboo forest with dappled sunlight. 10 seconds. Aspect ratio: 9:16. \nResolution: 720p. 2D anime illustration style, vibrant colors, \nStudio Ghibli inspired, cheerful atmosphere.\n```\n\n### Scene 3: Wide Landscape\n```\nCute chibi cartoon style, many adorable chubby giant pandas feasting \nand playing in a magical bamboo forest at golden hour. Pandas eating \nbamboo, climbing stalks, rolling around happily. Giant bamboo stalks \ntowering overhead. Ground covered with bamboo leaves and stalks. \nWarm golden sunset light filtering through bamboo. A wooden sign says \n竹子最好吃. 10 seconds. Aspect ratio: 9:16. Resolution: 720p. \n2D anime illustration style, vibrant warm colors, Studio Ghibli inspired, \nmagical whimsical mood.\n```\n\n## Consistency Checklist\n\nWhen generating multiple scenes, ensure:\n\n- [ ] Same art style across all scenes\n- [ ] Same character design (size, proportions, colors)\n- [ ] Consistent color palette\n- [ ] Similar lighting quality and color temperature\n- [ ] Matching resolution and aspect ratio\n- [ ] Coherent environment (same world/location)\n- [ ] Compatible level of detail\n\n## Tips for Better Results\n\n### DO\n✅ Use specific, descriptive language\n✅ Mention art style explicitly\n✅ Include emotional tone\n✅ Describe lighting and colors\n✅ Specify aspect ratio and resolution\n✅ Use reference to first image for subsequent scenes\n✅ Include duration/timing context\n\n### DON'T\n❌ Use vague terms (\"nice\", \"good\", \"pretty\")\n❌ Reference copyrighted characters\n❌ Forget aspect ratio\n❌ Mix conflicting art styles\n❌ Use low-contrast descriptions\n❌ Assume consistency without specifying\n\n## Reference Image Strategy\n\nFor maximum consistency:\n\n1. **Generate Scene 1** with full detailed prompt\n2. **Reference Scene 1** in Scene 2 prompt:\n   ```\n   \"Same art style and character design as [Scene 1 description]...\"\n   ```\n3. **Reference Scene 1** in Scene 3 prompt:\n   ```\n   \"Maintain visual consistency with previous scenes. Same chibi pandas, \n   same art style, same color palette...\"\n   ```\n\n## Common Pitfalls\n\n| Problem | Solution |\n|---------|----------|\n| Inconsistent characters | Repeat exact character description in each prompt |\n| Clashing art styles | Use identical art style phrase in all prompts |\n| Wrong aspect ratio | Always specify \"Aspect ratio: 9:16\" |\n| Blurry/low-quality | Specify \"Resolution: 720p\" and \"high quality\" |\n| Inconsistent lighting | Use same lighting description (e.g., \"warm golden hour\") |\n| Wrong composition | Be explicit about camera angle and framing |\n\n## Aspect Ratio Specifics\n\n### 9:16 (Portrait - YouTube Shorts)\n- Width: 720px, Height: 1280px\n- Good for: Close-ups, vertical compositions, mobile viewing\n- Prompt: \"Aspect ratio: 9:16. Resolution: 720p.\"\n\n### 16:9 (Landscape - Traditional)\n- Width: 1280px, Height: 720px\n- Good for: Wide landscapes, cinematic shots\n- Prompt: \"Aspect ratio: 16:9. Resolution: 720p.\"\n
