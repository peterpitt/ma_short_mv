# Music Prompting Guide for Animated Shorts\n\nCraft effective prompts for generating background music that enhances your animated shorts.\n\n## Music Structure for 30-Second Shorts\n\n### Three-Act Structure\n\n```\n[0:00-0:08] Intro (8 seconds)\n- Establish mood and theme\n- Light instrumentation\n- Curious or inviting tone\n\n[0:08-0:22] Main (14 seconds)\n- Build energy or narrative\n- Full instrumentation\n- Peak emotional moment around 0:16\n\n[0:22-0:30] Outro (8 seconds)\n- Satisfying conclusion\n- Soften slightly or maintain energy\n- Prepare for next video or end screen\n```\n\n## Prompt Template\n\n```\n[Mood] [genre] background music for [content type].\n[Instrumentation]. [Tempo]. [Emotional tone].\n[Structure with timestamps]. \nTotal duration: 30 seconds.\nNo vocals, instrumental only.\n```\n\n## Example Prompts\n\n### Cute Animals (Chibi Style)\n```\nPlayful and cheerful children's background music for cute panda animation.\nLight xylophone, ukulele, and soft percussion. Happy carefree mood.\nGentle tempo around 110 BPM.\n[0:00-0:08] Intro: soft xylophone melody begins, curious and gentle.\n[0:08-0:22] Main: ukulele joins, bouncy rhythm builds, full of joy.\n[0:22-0:30] Outro: melody softens, warm and satisfying ending.\nTotal duration: 30 seconds. Instrumental only, no vocals.\n```\n\n### Adventure/Action\n```\nDynamic and energetic background music for adventure animation.\nSynth leads, driving bass, electronic drums. Exciting and heroic mood.\nFast tempo around 140 BPM.\n[0:00-0:06] Intro: synth riff builds tension.\n[0:06-0:20] Main: full energy, drums kick in, driving forward momentum.\n[0:20-0:30] Outro: climax at 0:25, then quick resolution.\nTotal duration: 30 seconds. Instrumental only, no vocals.\n```\n\n### Relaxing/Ambient\n```\nCalm and soothing background music for nature animation.\nPiano, strings, ambient pads. Peaceful and meditative mood.\nSlow tempo around 80 BPM.\n[0:00-0:10] Intro: soft piano melody, strings enter gently.\n[0:10-0:25] Main: full arrangement, consistent peaceful energy.\n[0:25-0:30] Outro: fade to gentle piano.\nTotal duration: 30 seconds. Instrumental only, no vocals.\n```\n\n## Instrumentation Guide\n\n### Playful/Whimsical\n- Xylophone (bright, bouncy)\n- Ukulele (warm, friendly)\n- Glockenspiel (magical, light)\n- Soft marimba (smooth, organic)\n- Bells/chimes (delicate, whimsical)\n\n### Action/Dramatic\n- Synth leads (modern, energetic)\n- Electric guitar (driving, powerful)\n- Strings (orchestral, intense)\n- Drums/percussion (rhythm, drive)\n- Bass (foundation, energy)\n\n### Calm/Meditative\n- Piano (elegant, emotional)\n- Strings (orchestral, lush)\n- Ambient pads (atmospheric, spacious)\n- Acoustic guitar (warm, organic)\n- Nature sounds (wind, water - optional)\n\n## Tempo Guidelines\n\n| BPM | Feeling | Use Case |\n|-----|---------|----------|\n| 60-80 | Slow, meditative | Relaxing, emotional content |\n| 90-110 | Moderate, gentle | Cute animals, slice-of-life |\n| 120-140 | Upbeat, energetic | Action, adventure, fun |\n| 140+ | Fast, intense | High-energy, exciting moments |\n\n## Best Practices\n\n1. **Avoid Recognizable Melodies**: Request \"original, non-recognizable melodic material\" to avoid copyright issues\n2. **No Artist References**: Never mention existing artists or song titles\n3. **Clear Structure**: Always specify timing for intro/main/outro\n4. **Emotional Clarity**: Be specific about mood (not just \"happy\" but \"playful and curious\")\n5. **Instrumentation Mix**: Combine 2-4 instruments for richness without muddiness\n6. **Volume Dynamics**: Mention if music should build, plateau, or fade\n7. **No Vocals**: Always specify \"instrumental only, no vocals\" unless vocals are desired\n\n## Common Mistakes to Avoid\n\n❌ \"Make it sound like [Famous Song]\" → ✅ \"Upbeat, bouncy melody with xylophone and ukulele\"\n❌ \"Background music\" (too vague) → ✅ \"Cheerful, playful children's music with gentle tempo\"\n❌ \"Happy music\" (too generic) → ✅ \"Playful and curious mood, light instrumentation\"\n❌ Forgetting duration → ✅ \"Total duration: 30 seconds\"\n❌ Forgetting \"no vocals\" → ✅ \"Instrumental only, no vocals\"\n\n## Synchronization Tips\n\n### Music Beats Align with Scene Changes\n- Scene 1 (0-10s): Intro + Main beginning\n- Scene 2 (10-20s): Main peak energy\n- Scene 3 (20-30s): Main + Outro\n\n### Text Overlay Timing\n- Title/Hook: During intro (0-8s) with building music\n- Scene descriptions: During main (8-22s) with full energy\n- CTA: During outro (22-30s) with satisfying conclusion\n\n## Post-Generation Adjustments\n\nIf generated music needs tweaking:\n- Use `ffmpeg` to adjust volume: `-filter:a \"volume=0.8\"`\n- Trim to exact duration: `atrim=0:30`\n- Fade in/out: `afade=t=in:st=0:d=1,afade=t=out:st=29:d=1`\n
